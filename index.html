Questions, answered

How does it work?

Sign in with your @g.ucla.edu Google account (the Google version of your @ucla.edu email), add your classes, and you're in a group chat for each one. No new password to remember.

Is BChat an official UCLA app?

No. BChat is an independent project built by UCLA students. It isn't affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by UCLA.

Who can join?

Current UCLA students, signing in with their @g.ucla.edu Google account. If you're under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission.

Is it free?

Yes. BChat is free, with no ads.

What can other students see about me?

Your display name, profile picture, and what you post in chats you're both in. Your email address, class year, and major aren't shown to other students. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report someone or delete my account?

Tap a person's name or long-press one of their messages to report or block them. To delete your account, go to Settings → Delete account.

When can I download it?

BChat is coming to the App Store soon. Email bchatdevx@gmail.com if you'd like to help test it early.
